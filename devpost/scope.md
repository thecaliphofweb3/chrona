# Chrona

A calm, motivational morning planner that helps people prepare their schedule, meals, and outfit in one place.

## The Unique Kernel

Chrona pairs an encouraging “good morning” moment with a practical daily plan. Instead of stopping at a motivational quote, it helps someone organise the day ahead—what they will do, eat, and wear—so they can begin feeling prepared.

## Who It's For

People whose mornings feel stressful because they are deciding what to wear, eat, and make time for. Today, they may make each decision separately or leave it until the last minute; this app gives them one simple starting point.

## The Core Loop

Each morning, a person opens the app, receives a good-morning greeting and motivational quote, then sets or reviews their schedule, meals, and outfit. They leave with their daily plan visibly set and a short piece of encouraging advice, giving them a reason to return the next morning.

## Inspiration & Identity

The experience should feel calm, supportive, and motivating—like a short morning ritual rather than a demanding productivity tool. It begins with a warm greeting and a quote from a notable person; no visual references have been chosen yet.

## Why This Matters to the Learner

Mornings are the learner's hardest time to organise. They want to make something that helps other people face the same mix of decision fatigue and preparation.

## What "Working" Looks Like

In a short screen demo, a person opens the app and sees a greeting and quote. They set a timed daily schedule with alarms, choose breakfast, lunch, and dinner while seeing calorie information, then upload or select clothing images for headwear, eyewear, top, bottom, and footwear. They choose a mannequin body shape—standard, slim, or plus-size—and see the selected outfit displayed on it. The finished daily plan and a short piece of advice are shown together.

## The POC Boundary

- A good-morning greeting and motivational quote.
- One-day schedule planning with timed entries and alarms.
- Breakfast, lunch, and dinner planning with calorie information.
- A wardrobe input organised into five categories: headwear, eyewear, top, bottom, and footwear.
- A visual mannequin that displays the selected clothing and supports standard, slim, and plus-size shapes.
- A completed-plan view with short, non-medical encouraging advice.

## Later

- Account sign-in and saving plans across many days.
- A larger recipe and food database, grocery planning, or tailored nutrition support.
- Device notifications that work when the app is closed.
- More clothing categories, weather-aware recommendations, and sharing outfits.

## Explicitly Cut

- Judging whether clothes will physically fit: this requires a person's and each garment's measurements, so the proof of concept is a visual preview only.
- Medical, diet, or health advice: calorie information and encouragement are enough for the first version; personalised health guidance needs reliable data and careful safeguards.
- Photorealistic virtual try-on: displaying chosen clothing on a simple mannequin proves the outfit-planning idea without turning the project into a separate image-generation project.
